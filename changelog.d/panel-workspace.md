## Changed

- Reader's workspace is rebuilt on a new panel library. Tabs can be split beside or below each other, dragged between panes, and laid out with side regions; each window keeps its own layout. Layouts saved by earlier versions are not carried over.
- A source's annotations, note and citation are three Notes panels that follow the document in front. Pinning one keeps it on its source as a tab of its own. On a phone they are screens of the source's tab.
- The library has one search box. It filters by title, author and tag, and lists matches in notes and annotations above the table.
- Annotations for the whole collection are shown in the same list as a source's annotations, grouped by source, with the same filters and "Copy as Markdown".
- "Export citations" is one command: it exports the selected sources, or the view in front when nothing is selected.
- The display menu has text size, line length, typeface and theme. Language, density, annotation ownership inside a chapter, text selection and the default highlight colour are in Settings.

## Removed

- The saved-page text index and searching inside saved pages. PDFs keep the viewer's own find.
- Thumbnails and the cards view of the library.
- The annotations table and its saved views.
- The "Sidebar while reading" setting. The Sources sidebar is shown or hidden only by its toggle.
- The commands "Open annotations", "Open source note", "Open citation" and their "beside" variants, and the pane commands "Reset panes", "Move tab to other pane", "Arrange side by side" and "Arrange top and bottom".
