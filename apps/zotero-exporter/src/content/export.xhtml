Your personal library

Requires Zotero 10.0.x and a signed-in Zotero account.

This version exports all of My Library. Group libraries and trashed items are excluded. Finish syncing first and avoid editing Zotero during the export.

This exporter does not upload files, connect your account to Reader or change your Zotero library. The folder may contain private or copyrighted material; choose where to save it carefully.

Destination

A new, uniquely named folder will be created inside it. Existing folders are never overwritten.
