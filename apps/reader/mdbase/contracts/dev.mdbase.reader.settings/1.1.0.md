# mdbase Reader settings

The collection's settings record for mdbase Reader, at
`_settings/dev.mdbase.reader.md`. It also implements `mdbase.app-settings`, whose
reserved fields (`app`, `scope`, `settings_version`) it shares.

## Name templates

`source_name_template` and `annotation_name_template` are
[Knap](https://knap.md) templates that choose the preferred file name of a new
source note or annotation. Reader always makes the result safe for every file
system, and falls back to its own names when the result is empty, unusable or
taken. A name is chosen once, when the record is created: changing a template
never renames existing files.

Source note templates may contain `/` to put notes in folders under `sources/`.
Annotations always go directly under `annotations/`.

Variables for source notes: `title`, `authors`, `author` (the first author's family
name), `year`, `type`, `publisher`, `site`, `id`, `short_id` and `date`, plus
`parent_title`, `parent_author`, `parent_year` and `parent_stem` for chapters.

Variables for annotations: `id`, `short_id`, `type`, `quote`, `words`, `page`,
`color`, `date` and `source_stem`.

Filters: `slug`, `lower`, `upper`, `capitalize`, `title`, `trim`, `truncate`,
`replace`, `split`, `join`, `first`, `last`, `slice`, `length` and `date`.

## Highlight colours

Reader has five highlight colours: `yellow`, `green`, `blue`, `pink` and `purple`.
An annotation stores only its colour.

`highlight_colors` lists the colours Reader offers when making or recolouring a
highlight, in the order it offers them. It has at least one colour and none twice;
Reader ignores colours it does not know. A colour left out is not removed: its
highlights still draw in that colour, show in filters and can be recoloured. When
the colour last used is left out, Reader makes new highlights in the first colour
listed. Without the field, Reader offers all five in the order above.

`highlight_meanings` gives a colour a short label, at most 40 characters, such as
`pink: Question`. Because highlights store only their colour, changing a meaning
relabels every highlight already in that colour. A colour without a meaning is
called by its name.

## Versions

1.1.0 adds `highlight_colors` and `highlight_meanings` to 1.0.0. Both are optional,
so every 1.0.0 record is a 1.1.0 record, and `settings_version` stays 1.

## Authority

This artifact is passive and grants nothing.
